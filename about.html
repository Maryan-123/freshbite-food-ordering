Our Story

Bringing Somalia's Best Food to Your Doorstep

FreshBite started in 2021 with a simple idea: everyone deserves access to great local food without the wait. What began as a small delivery team working with five restaurants in Mogadishu has grown into a platform connecting thousands of hungry customers with the flavors they love - from family-run Somali kitchens to modern international cuisine.

Our Mission

Make ordering great food simple, fast, and reliable for every customer and every restaurant partner we work with.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted food-delivery platform in East Africa, championing local restaurants and home-grown flavors.

Our Values

Freshness, fairness to our restaurant partners, respect for our delivery riders, and honest, transparent pricing.
